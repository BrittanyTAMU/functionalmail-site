Privacy

Functional Mail is a mail service of Marcus Facilities LLC. This page covers functionalmail.com. Invited people can sign in to an inbox. This website does not open accounts to the public.

Last updated October 14, 2026.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we ask for your first name and email address. That is so we can tell you when accounts become available.

The form sends those two answers to Google Forms, which stores them for us. We use them only for early-access notes. We do not sell them, and we do not add you to a marketing blast.

If you email admin@functionalmail.com, we use that message to reply.

Cookies and extras

This website does not run advertising or analytics cookies. It is a simple set of pages. Google may use its own cookies when the waitlist form is submitted to Google Forms.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist answers until accounts open and the note has been sent, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and the waitlist is meant for adults.

Ask us to remove you

Email admin@functionalmail.com from the address you signed up with and say you’d like to be removed. We’ll take you off the list.
